Campaign Platform

Better Communication Between Leadership Team and Student Body

As Vice Chair, I would facilitate better communication between leadership team and the student body. I would accomplish this through the use of newsletters and the Villa Square that give detailed updates on everything leadership team is working on, including student advocacy projects, events planning, commnity meeting and more. This way, students know what leadership team is doing for them, and they can easily propose an join projects they find interesting without necessarily having to be a full member, allowing for leadership involvement from the wider community

More support for student-led clubs and initiatives

Students at KLS are changemaker -- they all have amazing ideas. As Vice Chair, I would ensure that even individual students can get leadership team support for their initiatives, as well as student clubs.

Orientation Program

Orientation Week is one of the school year's most important. Its where we meet our new classmates, make new connections, and see our old friends. if elected I would work to create an orientation program where we foster a lot of cross-grade interaction, initiate our senior-freshman peer mentor system, and introduce our school in an effective, yet fun way, both fostering and easing new and returning students in our community.

Create Comprehensive Student Support Systems

In addition to the Peer Mentoring system, I would also work to implement more Student Support Systems, particularly related to both academic and community opportunities, such as volunteering, and AP/SAT study groups, and Bio10/Chem25 prep sessions.
